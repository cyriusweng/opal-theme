A place for passages, questions and useful connections.

## Keep the source nearby

Write down the author, title and page before a quotation becomes separated from the book. Put your own response beneath it, where you can see how the thought began.

## Leave a question

What would change this reading? A counterexample, another chapter, a different translation? Keep a small question ready for the next visit.
